# Jasmin Knowledge Hub Card / CMS Length Rules

Status: permanent application-owned Knowledge Hub instruction

These are hard maximums and must be checked before saving:

- Article title: 60 characters maximum
- Excerpt: 149 characters maximum

Do not exceed either limit.

If an edit or strengthened article goes over:

- shorten the title without changing its primary search intent;
- shorten the excerpt without removing the core customer benefit;
- do not remove CMS fields;
- do not alter unrelated metadata or links.

Always report:

- final title character count;
- final excerpt character count.

These limits protect the Knowledge Hub card layout from being stretched. They apply to new articles and to edits/strengthening of existing articles before the CRM article is saved or sent to Wix CMS.
